Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026.

GTA 6 Brasil Publisher ("the app") is a publishing tool used by the creator team of GTA 6 Brasil (@gta6hoje), a Brazilian fan channel about GTA 6 with no affiliation with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. It lets the creator review, schedule and publish their own videos to the TikTok account they connect.

Information we access

  • TikTok profile information (display name, username and avatar), through Login Kit, only to show which account is connected.
  • Creator settings returned by the Content Posting API (allowed privacy options, interaction settings and maximum video length), only to build the posting screen.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok after the creator authorizes the app.

How we use it

Only to publish the videos that the creator reviews and approves in the app, with the privacy and interaction settings the creator chooses for each post. We do not post anything the creator has not approved.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and approvals are stored only on the creator team's own computer. Videos are uploaded directly from that computer to TikTok. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your control

You can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions). When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working, and we delete them on request.

Contact

GTA 6 Brasil · @gta6hoje on TikTok and YouTube.

Política de privacidade

O GTA 6 Brasil Publisher é a ferramenta que a equipe do GTA 6 Brasil (@gta6hoje), canal de fã sem vínculo com a Rockstar Games ou a Take-Two Interactive, usa para revisar, agendar e publicar os próprios vídeos na conta do TikTok conectada.

Usamos só o nome, o @ e a foto do perfil (para mostrar qual conta está conectada), as configurações de postagem da conta e os tokens de acesso. Tudo fica guardado no computador da equipe, nada é vendido nem compartilhado, e só é publicado o que o criador aprova, com a privacidade que ele escolhe. Para desconectar, use as permissões de apps nas configurações do TikTok.

GTA 6 Brasil Publisher

The publishing tool of GTA 6 Brasil (@gta6hoje), a Brazilian fan channel about GTA 6. The creator connects their TikTok account, reviews each video and its caption, chooses who can view it and which interactions are allowed, and the app publishes it at the scheduled time.

A ferramenta de publicação do GTA 6 Brasil: o criador conecta a conta do TikTok, revisa cada vídeo, escolhe quem pode ver e o app publica no horário agendado.
